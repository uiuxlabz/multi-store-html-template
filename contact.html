Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Quick answers to the things we hear most. Can't find what you need? Send us a message above.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the US. Express shipping (1-2 days) is available at checkout for $9.99. International orders typically arrive within 7-14 business days depending on your location.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy on most items. Items must be in their original condition with tags attached. Simply log into your account, go to Order History, and select the item you'd like to return. We'll provide a prepaid shipping label.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight. Note that customs duties and import taxes may apply depending on your country's regulations.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number and link. You can also view your order status anytime by logging into your account and navigating to "My Orders" in the dashboard.
